Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

SaltShack is developed and published by Benjamin Secret, who is responsible for the personal information described in this policy.

SaltShack respects your privacy and uses information to provide app functionality, protect the service and support optional sharing. Personal information is not used for advertising, cross-app tracking or marketing.

Player Profiles and Leaderboards

SaltShack uses Google Firebase services for player profiles and leaderboards. An anonymous guest player identifier may be created automatically so that scores and profile information can be associated with the same player.

The app may store a generated or user-selected username, avatar selection, total points, game scores, streaks and other leaderboard-related progress on Firebase servers.

Usernames, avatars, scores and rankings may be visible to other users through the app’s leaderboards. User-selected usernames are subject to an automated profanity and abuse filter.

Optional Achievement Sharing

Achievement sharing defaults to “Only me”. If you select “Everyone”, selected new personal high scores, leaderboard improvements and level-ups may be uploaded to Firebase for inclusion in a public achievement feed.

Uploaded information includes your anonymous player identifier, username, selected avatar identifier, achievement type, relevant score, rank or level, and achievement and publication timestamps. Technical story identifiers and a significance score are also stored to combine updates and help organise the feed.

Public feed features may initially be available only to authorised testers. Shared achievements may subsequently be displayed to other app users as public feed functionality becomes available.

Collection photos, collection notes, quiz answers and private collection content are not included in public achievement uploads. Routine quiz completion and checklist activity remain in your personal activity history rather than being uploaded as public stories.

You can withdraw from achievement sharing by selecting “Only me”. Once the app connects to Firebase, this stops further achievement uploads and removes your existing shared achievement stories. Your local achievement history remains on your device.

The achievement-sharing setting does not hide your separate leaderboard profile, scores or rankings. The setting is currently stored on each device, so check your choice on each device you use.

Username Reports and Moderation

Users can report an inappropriate public username from within the app. A report may include the reporting player’s anonymous Firebase user identifier, the reported player’s anonymous Firebase user identifier, the reported username, the reason for the report and the time it was submitted.

Reports are used for moderation, abuse prevention and protecting the service. Reports are not publicly accessible to other app users.

When a username is reported, the app may also block that player’s identifying profile information on the reporting user’s device. The blocked player’s rank and score may remain visible so leaderboard positions stay accurate. The block preference is stored locally and can be removed from the Blocked Players screen.

Data Stored on Your Device

Quiz progress, preferences, bookmarks, collection entries, user-added mineral notes, user-selected photos and personal achievement history are stored locally on your device. Selected progress information may also be uploaded for the profile, leaderboard and optional achievement features described above.

Photos and text added to My Collection are not uploaded to Firebase by SaltShack. If you choose to export, share or back up information, the resulting files are handled by the destination or service you select.

Accounts

No account registration, email address, phone number or password is required to use SaltShack. Cloud player functionality uses an automatically generated anonymous guest profile rather than a conventional sign-in account.

“Anonymous” means you do not need to provide a real name or contact details. The player identifier still links your cloud profile, scores and any shared achievements to the same player. Avoid including personal or sensitive information in your username.

How and Why We Use Information

We use player information to provide profiles, score syncing, leaderboards and other requested app functionality. Where this processing is necessary to provide the service you use, our lawful basis is performance of our contract with you.

Optional public achievement sharing is based on your consent, expressed by selecting “Everyone” after being told what will be shared. You can withdraw that consent by selecting “Only me”. Withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before withdrawal.

We use information for proportionate abuse prevention, username moderation and service protection on the basis of our legitimate interests in maintaining a safe and reliable app. Information may also be retained where necessary to meet applicable legal obligations.

Data Retention and Deletion

Cloud-hosted player profile and leaderboard information is retained while the anonymous player profile remains active. You can delete your cloud player profile from the Profile screen in the app.

Profile deletion removes the associated Firebase player record, username reservation, shared achievement stories and anonymous Firebase Authentication account. Shared stories are withdrawn before the authentication account is deleted. Deletion requires connectivity; if it fails, the app may ask you to try again.

A minimal private feed-control record containing the former player identifier, a private-sharing status and an update timestamp may remain after profile deletion. It does not contain the deleted achievement stories.

Shared achievement stories are retained until you withdraw sharing, delete your cloud player profile, or the stories are removed through service maintenance or moderation. There is currently no fixed automatic expiry period for shared stories.

Previously downloaded copies of public stories may remain on other devices until their stored content is refreshed or removed. SaltShack cannot recall copies independently saved by other people.

Deleting your cloud player profile does not delete local collection photos, mineral notes, bookmarks or other information stored on your device. You may create a new anonymous player profile later.

Moderation reports may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to investigate abuse, enforce moderation rules, protect the service or meet applicable legal obligations.

Third-Party Services and International Processing

SaltShack uses Google Firebase for anonymous authentication, cloud-hosted player profiles, leaderboards and optional achievement sharing. Google processes information to provide these services under its applicable Firebase terms and data-processing arrangements.

Firebase processing may take place outside your country, including outside the United Kingdom and European Economic Area. Applicable international-transfer arrangements depend on the Firebase service and processing location. You can contact us for information about the arrangements relevant to your data.

Further information is available on Firebase’s privacy and security page .

Your Choices and Rights

You can choose whether to share eligible achievements, withdraw achievement sharing, change your username or avatar, and delete your cloud player profile using the app’s controls.

You can continue using the app’s local reference and collection features without an active cloud player profile.

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, you may have rights to access your personal information, correct inaccurate information, request deletion, restrict processing, obtain a portable copy of information, and withdraw consent.

Right to object: you may object to processing based on legitimate interests, including processing for service protection and moderation. We will consider your request in accordance with applicable data protection law.

To exercise your rights, contact us using the address below. Because profiles do not require contact details, we may need your player identifier or other proportionate information to locate the relevant records and verify your request. Do not send passwords.

You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office or the relevant data protection authority where you live.

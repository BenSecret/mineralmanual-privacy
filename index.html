Privacy Policy

Mineral Manual respects your privacy and collects only the information necessary to provide certain app features.

Player Profiles and Leaderboards

Mineral Manual uses Firebase services to provide player profiles and leaderboards. When the app is used, an anonymous user identifier may be created automatically.

The app may store a generated or user-selected username, avatar selection, total points, game scores, streaks, and other leaderboard-related progress on Firebase servers.

Usernames, avatars, scores, and rankings may be visible to other users through the app's leaderboards. User-selected usernames are subject to an automated profanity filter.

This information is used only to provide app functionality such as player profiles, score syncing, and leaderboards. It is not used for advertising, cross-app tracking, or marketing.

Data Stored on Your Device

Quiz progress, preferences, bookmarks, collection entries, user-added mineral notes, and user-selected photos are stored locally on the user's device.

Photos and text added to My Collection remain on the user's device and are not transmitted to external servers by Mineral Manual.

Accounts

No account registration, email address, phone number, or password is required to use the app.

Third-Party Services

Mineral Manual uses Google Firebase for anonymous authentication and cloud-hosted leaderboard functionality. Data transmitted to Firebase is used to provide these app features.
